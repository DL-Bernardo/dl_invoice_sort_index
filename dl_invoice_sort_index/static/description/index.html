❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Answers to common administrative and technical questions.

Why does standard Odoo sort invoices alphabetically instead of numerically?

Standard Odoo stores invoice numbers in a text string (VARCHAR) field name. Databases sort text character-by-character, meaning '10' comes before '2'. This module extracts the numerical part into an indexed integer column to restore natural human and audit sequencing.

Does this module alter my legal invoice numbers or fiscal hashes?

No. Your invoice numbers, prefixes, series, and certified electronic signatures remain 100% untouched. The module only adds an auxiliary sorting field to instruct Odoo views and exports on the correct numerical order.

Is this module compatible with custom prefixes like "FT 2025/001" or "NC 2025/1"?

Yes. The parsing logic dynamically identifies and extracts the numeric sequence at the end of the invoice string, regardless of prefix formatting.

Is it compatible with Odoo.sh and multi-company setups?

Yes, 100%. Tested and verified on Odoo.sh, dedicated cloud servers, and multi-company database environments.
